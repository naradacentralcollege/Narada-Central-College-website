Celebrating Every Child, Inspiring Every Future

World Children’s Day 2026 is celebrated under the inspiring theme “Strong Minds – Enriched Children.” This year’s theme highlights the importance of nurturing children’s mental well-being, confidence, creativity, and personal development.

Children are the future of our nation. Every child deserves a safe, caring, and supportive environment where they can learn, express their ideas, develop their talents, and achieve their dreams. Along with education and knowledge, children need love, encouragement, understanding, and emotional security to grow into confident and responsible citizens.

As a school community, we recognize our responsibility to protect children’s rights, respect their voices, encourage their abilities, and guide them towards a brighter future. Parents, teachers, and society all have an important role to play in building strong minds and enriching young lives.

On this special occasion, we extend our warmest wishes to all our beloved students. May every child grow with courage, kindness, wisdom, and confidence to make the world a better place.

Happy World Children’s Day 2026!

Honouring Our Teachers, Inspiring Our Future

Our school celebrated Teachers’ Day on October 6, 2026, with a special programme organized by the School Prefect Board to honour and appreciate the dedication, commitment, and invaluable service of our teachers.

The programme was organized to express students’ gratitude and respect for the teachers who guide, inspire, and support them throughout their educational journey. Through this meaningful celebration, the students recognized the important role teachers play in shaping their knowledge, character, values, and future aspirations.

The School Prefect Board took the initiative in organizing this special occasion, demonstrating leadership, teamwork, and appreciation for the teaching profession. The celebration created a valuable opportunity for students to express their heartfelt thanks to their beloved teachers.

A teacher’s influence goes far beyond the classroom. Their guidance and encouragement help students discover their potential and become responsible citizens of tomorrow.

Our heartfelt appreciation goes to all our teachers for their tireless efforts, patience, and dedication to education.

Happy Teachers’ Day to all our respected teachers!

Celebrating Excellence, Inspiring Champions!

Narada Central College is proud to celebrate the outstanding achievement of our women's Elle team in winning the All-Island championship. This remarkable victory is a proud moment for our school and the entire school community.

The achievement reflects the dedication, determination, teamwork, and perseverance of our talented players. Through their commitment and continuous efforts, the team demonstrated outstanding sportsmanship and brought honour to our beloved college.

This success would not have been possible without the guidance of the coaches, the encouragement of the teachers, and the support of the school administration, parents, and fellow students. Their contribution played an important role in motivating the team towards this achievement.

This All-Island victory will serve as an inspiration to other students to pursue their sporting dreams with confidence, discipline, and determination.

Our heartfelt congratulations go to every player and everyone who contributed to this wonderful achievement. We are immensely proud of our champions!

Congratulations to our All-Island Women's Elle Champions!
