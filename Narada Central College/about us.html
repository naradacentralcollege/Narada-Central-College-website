Proud History (A Glorious Heritage)

Narada Central College, Parakaduwa, has played a significant role in the advancement of education in the Sabaragamuwa Province of the Ratnapura District. Its proud history spans more than six decades and is marked by numerous remarkable milestones.

Officially established on 1 September 1959, the school initially worked in collaboration with Parakaduwa Junior School, providing education to children in the surrounding area. Later that same year, it became a senior school. From 1969 onwards, the college successfully conducted G.C.E. Ordinary Level classes in all three streams: Science, Arts, and Commerce. In 1979, a primary section was added to the college. During the 1980s and 1990s, its physical infrastructure was further strengthened with the construction of two two-storey buildings. As a result of the "Mahapola" exhibition held in 1986, a protective boundary wall was also constructed around the school playground.

Elevation to Central College Status (Grade 1C)

Marking a golden milestone in the history of the college, the school was officially designated a "Central College" in 1986. With this achievement, it was classified as a Grade 1C school and commenced Advanced Level classes in the Arts and Commerce streams. This opened up valuable opportunities for many students in the region to pursue their dreams of entering university.

Introduction of the Science Stream and Grade 1AB Excellence

With the aim of producing students equipped with the technological and scientific knowledge required for the modern era, the Advanced Level Science stream was introduced in 2013. Today, under the national "Thousand Secondary Schools Project," the college continues to develop comprehensively and proudly stands as a prestigious Grade 1AB school offering all major subject streams.

Key Milestones in the College's Journey

  • 1959.09.01 Establishment of the college in collaboration with Parakaduwa Junior School
  • 1969 - 1979 Development into a senior school and commencement of Ordinary Level classes in Science, Arts, and Commerce
  • 1979 - 1990 Establishment of the primary section and construction of two two-storey buildings
  • 1986 Elevation to Central College status (Grade 1C) and commencement of Advanced Level classes
  • 2013 - Present Introduction of the Advanced Level Science stream and development into a leading Grade 1AB school

Message from the Principal

M. S. M. Ranaweera

Principal – Narada Central College, Parakaduwa

As the Principal of Narada Central College, Parakaduwa, I am honoured to lead an institution that has been dedicated to educating and nurturing generations of students for more than six decades. Our college takes great pride in its rich history, strong educational foundation, and commitment to shaping the future of our children.

At Narada Central College, we believe that education goes beyond academic achievement. Our mission is to develop well-rounded individuals who possess knowledge, discipline, good character, creativity, and a sense of responsibility towards society. We strive to provide a safe, supportive, and inspiring learning environment where every student is encouraged to discover their talents and reach their full potential.

Our dedicated teachers, supportive parents, committed staff, and valued past pupils play an important role in the continued development of our college. Through their collective efforts, we aim to strengthen our academic standards, encourage participation in sports and co-curricular activities, and nurture the leadership qualities of our students.

We believe that the students of today are the leaders of tomorrow. Therefore, we are committed to instilling in our students the values of respect, honesty, compassion, unity, and perseverance. By combining academic excellence with moral values and practical skills, we hope to prepare them to face the challenges of a rapidly changing world with confidence and integrity.

As we move forward, Narada Central College remains dedicated to continuous improvement, innovation, and educational excellence. Together, let us work towards a brighter future for our students and our beloved college, upholding its proud heritage while embracing new opportunities for growth and achievement.

I extend my sincere gratitude to all members of our school community for their continued support, dedication, and cooperation in fulfilling this noble mission.

M. S. M. Ranaweera
Principal
Narada Central College, Parakaduwa
